The Band

Meet the six of us.

C

Vocalist

C brings the high notes and the energy. When she’s not singing, she’s probably thinking about the next riff.

W

Vocalist

W’s voice cuts through the mix. She and C handle the melodies and the shouts.

Q

Drums

Q keeps the beat and the chaos in check. If the room is shaking, that’s probably the kit.

H

Electric Guitar

H’s on one of the guitars—riffs, solos, and the occasional feedback.

G

Electric Guitar

G handles the other guitar. Together with H, they fill out the sound.

B

Bass

B holds down the low end and keeps everything locked with the drums.
